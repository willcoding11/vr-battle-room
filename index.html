SETTINGS

BATTLE ROOM

A miniature battle in a box in front of you. Fly out of one side of the box and you come back in the opposite side. Two modes:

FIGHTER: fly the cyan ship yourself and destroy the red waves. Enemies fight in groups: pincer pairs, flankers, snipers that hold range, heavies.
SQUADRON: a much bigger, zoomed-out battlefield with big floating "stars" (cover). You command 4 bot fighters: your right controller sets the direction they head in, and they fly themselves.

VR right handMirror steering (default): the ship (or, in Squadron, the squadron's heading) turns with your right controller, with gain (Fighter 2x, Squadron 3x), in any direction, including loops. Trigger: shoot (Squadron: hold = engage: the bots spread out a little and attack; release = regroup) · grip: boost (Squadron: only while not engaging) · A brake · click the stick to re-center · on the title: B switches mode, trigger starts
VR left handExtras: trigger shoots · grip boosts · stick forward boost / back brake · stick click resets the box · X+Y: settings (mode, steering, gains, turn speed, box size) · wrist shows score, hull / squadron status and radar
DesktopThe ship / squadron heading follows the mouse · Left click / Space shoot (Squadron: hold to engage) · Shift boost · F brake · N switch mode · M cycles steering · +/- box size · C reset box · right-drag or arrow keys look · Tab settings · R restart

On a Quest: press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page, then B picks the mode and the right trigger starts.
