BATTLE ROOM

A miniature battle in a box in front of you. Fly the cyan ship, destroy the red waves. Fly out of one side of the box and you come back in the opposite side.

VR right handMirror steering (default): the ship points where your right controller points. Hold the controller comfortably - its direction at game start = straight into the box. Click the right stick to re-center. No buttons needed while flying.
VR left handTrigger: shoot (and start/restart) · stick forward boost / back brake · grip + drag moves the box · grip + stick up/down resizes it · stick click resets it · X+Y: settings (steering mode, turn speed, box size) · wrist shows score + radar
