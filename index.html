SETTINGS

BATTLE ROOM

A miniature battle in a box in front of you. Fly out of one side of the box and you come back in the opposite side. The room changes shape (long, flat, tall or cube) every game and between rounds. Every round is a fresh battle: your side comes back at full strength, new star positions, and a smarter enemy. Reach the highest round you can. Messages show on your left wrist. Three modes:

FIGHTER: fly the cyan ship yourself and win round after round. Enemies fight in groups: pincer pairs, flankers, snipers that hold range, heavies.
SQUADRON: a much bigger, zoomed-out battlefield with big floating spherical "stars" (cover). You command 4 bot fighters: your right controller sets the direction they head in, and they fly themselves.
FLEET: an even bigger battle. You command 3 squadrons (blue, green, gold) and 2 capital ships against an enemy fleet. Destroy the enemy flagship to win the round; it's over only when both capital ships AND all your fighters are gone. LEFT hand points: trigger selects what you point at, stick click selects all, grip gives an order (point at a spot or star = hold/guard, at one of yours = follow, at an enemy = attack). The right hand steers the selection exactly like Squadron. Desktop: 1-5 select, 0 all, G order at the mouse.

VR right handMirror steering (default): the ship (or, in Squadron, the squadron's heading) turns with your right controller, with gain (Fighter 2x, Squadron 3x), in any direction, including loops.
